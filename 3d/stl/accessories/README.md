# Accessories

The side pieces twist‑lock into the keyholes on the head. The band's two
ends hook into the slots on top of a pair of cups, so every band needs two
cups. Builds without a band close the keyholes with plugs or pods.

## Side pieces

Print outer face down, no supports. The spring beam under the peg prints as
a 20 mm bridge, so keep the part fan on.

| File | What it is | Builds that use it |
|---|---|---|
| `cup_left.stl`, `cup_right.stl` | Plain headphone cups with a band slot on top | Every build with a band except Claude. Gamer uses only `cup_right` |
| `cup_mic.stl` | Left cup with a headset mic boom; the boom lies flat on the bed | Gamer |
| `cup_claude_left.stl`, `cup_claude_right.stl` | Cups with the Claude spark engraved 0.6 mm into the outer face | Claude |
| `plug.stl` | Closes a keyhole; fits either side, print two | Template, Skeleton |
| `pod_left.stl`, `pod_right.stl` | Astronaut comms pods, the right one with an antenna. `pod_left` is the same shape as `plug` | Astronaut |

## Bands

Print flat on the back face, no supports. The topper is part of the flat
outline, and so are the two end tabs with their hooks. To take a band off,
push an end in toward the head and lift it.
